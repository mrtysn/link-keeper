Why I rewrote my build in zsh

Sep 1, 2026
Share on X · Share on LinkedIn

For three years our build was a Makefile that nobody dared to touch. It had grown to nine hundred lines and every change broke something on somebody's machine.

So I rewrote it in zsh, one target at a time, with the old and the new running side by side until their outputs matched. The tool I used to compare them is difftool.

build() {
  print -- "building $1"
}

The new build is a third of the size and twice as fast, and new people can read it on their first day.
