Tags

    Filtered out

    What it is for: pages you keep open but never mean to read — a site's front page, your inbox, a calendar. A link matching a rule below is hidden from Links, Cards and every count, so it does not crowd the pile.

    Nothing is deleted: the link stays in its stash and gets the filtered-out tag. The Filtered out button in the bar above the list shows them;

    One rule per line, the address without https://. A rule matches that page only, never the pages under it: reddit.com is Reddit's front page, not a post. * stands for anything: mail.google.com/mail/u/*/#inbox is the inbox of any account, not an email in it. Right-click a page → Filter out this page adds its rule.
